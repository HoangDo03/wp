Về Bona

Since our founding in 1919, a constant thread has woven Bona’s foundation. Our values—passion, performance, and pioneering—shape everything we do, from our products to our company culture to our responsible business practices. It’s how we operate our business and engage with the world.

Our story

Bona’s founder, Wilhelm Edner, originally owned a small grocery shop in Malmö, Sweden where coffee was his favourite product.

In his shop, he also sold Bonvax, an agent which, when applied to a wood floor, created a polished and protective surface. Edner realised the potential of Bonvax, and founded Bona AB in 1919. Now, over 100 years later, Bona is the foremost leader in the hardwood floor industry, with a wealth of innovation experience and knowledge – all focused on bringing out the beauty in floors.

A global company headquartered in Sweden

With our head office in Malmö, Sweden, Bona is led by third and fourth generations of the Edner, Forsberg, and Brask family. Having started from humble beginnings, we are now a global organisation spanning more than 90 countries and represented by 17 subsidiaries, 70 distributors, 4 production sites, and more than 700 employees.

Our values

Passion

Inspiration. Devotion. Pride.

Passion is our foundation for doing business. We are passionate about what we do and how we do it. We are always fully committed to bringing out the beauty in floors. We take pride in our products, services, and systems and we strongly believe that working with passion is the best way to serve our customers and expand our business.

Performance

Quality. Safety. Reliability.

At Bona, performance means premium quality. We focus on the safety and satisfaction of craftsmen, customers, and floor owners. Through responsible solutions and shared knowledge, we empower customers to achieve the best possible results for every type of floor care. For us, performance is about delivering exceptional quality and service at every step.

Pioneering

Innovation. Creativity. Curiosity.

To pioneer is to push boundaries, and at Bona, it’s what we do best. We constantly reimagine what’s possible, transforming creative ideas into practical innovations. With courage and vision, we aim to stay ahead, delivering products and services that set new standards. For our customers, pioneering is the assurance of something better, every time.

Our promise

Every day, with every product and every interaction, we promise to offer products and experiences that bring out the beauty in floors.

From our commitment to sustainability, to our relationships with customers, to unparalleled service that provides peace of mind, we commit to delivering the absolute best, every time.

Sustainability

We believe that it's our responsibility to do better and be better in everything we do.

Renovating instead of renewing is the core of our company, driving our commitment to delivering more responsible solutions in the flooring industry. With over a century of expertise, we’ve learned that taking on this challenge fosters progress and inspires innovation across our company and in the solutions we create.
